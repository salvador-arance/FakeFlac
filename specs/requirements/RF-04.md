Estimate the cutoff frequency and issue a verdict: real, suspicious or fake.

The reported cutoff of a lossless file is where its content ends: a short, narrow-band sound below the treble (a content edge) is not reported as its cutoff (owner's report of 2026-11-21; detection v9, `specs/detection/rules.md` section 2, proposed for G1, ADR-0052). This changes no verdict.
