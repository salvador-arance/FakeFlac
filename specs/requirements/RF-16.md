priority: medium

For MP3 and M4A (AAC), flag files that were most likely transcoded from a full-band lossy streaming source (typically Opus from YouTube, e.g. a YouTube rip saved as MP3 320 kbps) by a cheap, deterministic indicator: a decoded sample rate of 48 kHz together with a cutoff at Opus's 20 kHz band edge. Such a file is `Suspicious` with its own reason, never `Fake`.

Source: owner's decision (2026-10-25), from the known limitation "Opus → MP3 (48 kHz)" of `specs/detection/lossy-source.md` section 8. Spec: `specs/detection/lossy-source.md` section 11 (rule L6a, detection v7, `detection-params.v7.toml`; acceptance K1–K7 in section 11.7), calibration report additions in `specs/calibration/lossy.md` section 6.8, contract `specs/contracts/commands.md` version 6, UI text in `specs/ui/lossy-formats.md`, ADR-0043.
