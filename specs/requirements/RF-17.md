---
id: RF-17
title: Explain a Fake verdict (the evidence that decided it, its strength, and the album context)
priority: medium
milestone: H2
status: specified
---

When a track is `Fake`, the app says **what evidence** made it `Fake` and **how strong** that evidence is, so the user can judge the verdict instead of trusting it blindly. Today a `Fake` carries an empty reasons list by design (rules V3, V4 and L3), while a `Suspicious` always has a reason. RF-17 adds only explanation: **it changes no verdict, reason, cutoff, estimate, measurement, threshold or version.** For the same file, verdict, cutoff and estimate are identical before and after.

Measurable acceptance (pending G1; `specs/detection/fake-evidence.md` section 8):
- **Evidence.** Every `TrackAnalysis` has an `evidence` list, not empty exactly when the verdict is `Fake`. A lossless `Fake` lists `gapStep` when rule V3's condition holds and `cutoffStep` when rule V4's holds (both when both hold); a lossy `Fake` lists `shortfall` and `cutoffStep` (rule L3). Each item carries the measured value, the `DetectionParams` threshold it was compared with, and the **margin** between them (never negative). No new parameter, no label, no new detection version.
- **Unchanged results.** Every existing detection, decoding, album, cache and CSV scenario passes unchanged; every fixture keeps its truth with `algorithmVersion` 8; `just accept` on the owner's corpus gives the same `summary.md` as before RF-17 (run metadata aside); the app's CSV export of the owner's library is byte-identical to the 2026-11-14 export.
- **Interface.** Selecting a `Fake` row shows, below the unchanged verdict line, one line per item with its margin, a sentence for the gap-only case ("The evidence is the gap pattern at 16 kHz, not the cutoff."), and an album note when the track is one of the `Fake` tracks of an album whose other analysed tracks are not all `Fake`. The last line of every `Fake` is the audio note: the traces are in the audio and cannot tell a converted file from a genuine release mastered from lossy audio. The State cell's tooltip shows the items. Exact English and Spanish texts in `specs/ui/fake-evidence.md`.
- **Compatibility.** Contract version 9 (a new field and two types; `reasons` unchanged). CSV unchanged (`csv_format_version` 1). Cached results written before RF-17 are reused except `Fake` rows without evidence, which are analysed again; no cache schema or engine change. The calibration tool's outputs do not change.
- **Manual.** The owner's walkthrough of six kinds of `Fake` in English and Spanish (M1) and the byte-identical library export (M2).

Source: owner's request (2026-11-14), from a whole-library run with the release app: of 501 lossless `Fake` tracks, about 38 % of those isolated in their albums lie within 3–4 dB of a threshold, against about 3 % of those in albums where most tracks are `Fake`, and the app shows both groups the same way; and 8 `Fake` tracks are database-verified rips of published CDs, so a `Fake` cannot claim that the file was converted (aggregates in `specs/detection/fake-evidence.md` section 1; no names, ADR-0022).
