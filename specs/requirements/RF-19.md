FakeFlac analyses every audio format that AudioAuditor 2.0.0 accepts: 35 extensions, of which RF-14 covers 5 (`.wav`, `.flac`, `.mp3`, `.m4a`, `.opus`). Each new format keeps RF-14's guarantees: decoded **without external tools** (no ffmpeg, no codec installed on the machine), deterministically (the same file gives the same `AudioProbe` everywhere, `EXTRACTION_VERSION`), and with the same result contract (ADR-0031).

The 30 extensions to add, by group (issue #204):

| Group | Extensions | Issue |
|---|---|---|
| Read by the current decoders | `.fla` `.bwf` `.m4b` `.m4r` `.mp4` `.alac` `.aac` | #206 |
| `symphonia` features not enabled yet | `.aiff` `.aif` `.ogg` (Vorbis) `.mp2` `.mka` `.webm` | #207 |
| In-house readers | `.rf64` `.au` `.snd` `.dsf` `.dff` | #208 |
| Lossless, no `symphonia` decoder | `.ape` `.wv` `.tta` `.tak` | #209 |
| Lossy, no `symphonia` decoder | `.wma` `.mpc` `.mp+` `.ac3` `.amr` `.3gp` `.3g2` `.spx` | #210 |

Measurable acceptance (to be fixed at G1 of each group's spec):
- **Coverage.** Each of the 35 extensions is analysed with the guarantees above, **or** its issue records the owner's reason not to support it (for example: no deterministic decoder with an acceptable licence exists). Archives and playlists are not audio formats and are out of scope.
- **Detection.** A new lossy codec gets a `[lossy.cutoffs]` table and a calibration setting before its source-bitrate verdict is trusted, or the spec limits it to the lossless-style evidence. DSD gets its own detection spec.
- **Budgets.** RNF-01, RNF-02 and RNF-06 hold with the new decoders; any new dependency has an ADR (licence, maintenance, `unsafe`/FFI).

Source: owner's decision of 2026-11-20 (H3 extension, #184): "every audio format that AudioAuditor covers should be covered by FakeFlac". The list comes from AudioAuditor's `SupportedFormats.cs` (2.0.0). RF-14 stays as specified for its five formats.

Spec: pending, one spec per group (#206 to #210); the surveys of #209 and #210 come first and are read-only.
