---
id: RF-21
title: Detect a previous lossy encoding from its quantisation fingerprint (double compression)
priority: medium
milestone: H3
status: draft
---

FakeFlac detects that a file went through a lossy encoder **even when no encoder low-pass is left to measure**, from the quantisation fingerprint that the encoder leaves in its transform domain. Re-analyse the decoded audio with the codec's transform, aligned to its frame grid, and the coefficients cluster on the quantiser's levels. The structure is absent at every other alignment and in audio that was never encoded. This covers what the cutoff rules (RF-04, RF-15) cannot see:

- a lossless file made from a lossy source encoded without a low-pass (`specs/detection/lossy-source.md`, "Sources without a low-pass");
- a lossy → lossy chain at close bitrates, or with a high low-pass (for example 128 → 320 kbps);
- a lossy origin hidden by resampling (for example an Opus rip resampled to 44.1 kHz, `lossy-source.md` section 11.6).

Measurable acceptance (to be fixed at G1 of its spec):
- **Coverage.** Each codec the spike marks as feasible (MP3 and AAC first; Opus and Vorbis if they qualify) has a rule, synthetic `@RF-21` scenarios and a calibration setting. Each codec that is not feasible is recorded as a known limitation with its measured reason.
- **False positives.** On the authentic lossless tracks of the labelled corpus, the new rule stays within the false-positive target of H3 (#186), counted the same way.
- **Gain.** Acceptance reports the labelled cases the rule catches that the previous detection version missed, as aggregates (ADR-0022).
- **Explained.** A verdict that relies on a fingerprint says so, with its codec and strength (RF-17), in both languages (RF-13), in the detail panel and the CSV.
- **Data, not code.** Every threshold and analysis length is in `DetectionParams` (ADR-0011, ADR-0025), with a new params version. Values fixed by the codec standards are named as such.
- **Budgets.** RNF-01 (10 % bound) and RNF-02 hold (`just perf`, `just memory`).
